# **App Name**: InFaith Journey

## Core Features:

- Secure User Authentication: Implements Firebase Authentication for distinct vendor and couple login flows, storing user data securely.
- Dynamic Vendor & Venue Browsing: Couples can view, search, and filter vendor and venue listings by categories, location, and price ranges, with real-time data fetched from Firestore.
- Vendor Application Portal: A comprehensive, multi-step application form for prospective vendors to submit business details, choose membership plans, and upload portfolio images, all recorded in Firestore.
- Admin Vendor Management Dashboard: An internal dashboard for Ricardo to efficiently review pending vendor applications, approve new listings, and manage existing vendor profiles and data within Firestore.
- Vendor Profile & Content Management: Approved vendors can securely log in to manage their own profile details, update service offerings, maintain photo galleries, and modify business information stored in Firestore.
- Membership Plan & Billing Integration: Manages Free, Standard, and Featured vendor membership plans, including integration with South African payment gateways like PayFast or Stripe for recurring ZAR payments, with status updates in Firestore.
- Inquiry & Quote Request System: Couples can send direct inquiries and request quotes to vendors via integrated forms, with these requests stored in Firestore and triggering email notifications.

## Style Guidelines:

- Background Color: Soft cream/beige (#F8F5F0). This very light, warm neutral hue creates a clean, spacious, and elegant canvas, forming the foundation of the high-end aesthetic.
- Primary Color: Dusty-rose (#C8A37E). This warm, muted rose tone is used for calls-to-action, interactive elements, and key highlights, conveying a sense of romance and sophisticated accent.
- Accent Color (Text): Warm dark neutral (#383633). A deep, desaturated charcoal for main body text and headings, ensuring high readability while maintaining a cohesive and warm color palette.
- Highlight Accent: Subtle gold (#E8DFB0). A very light, desaturated gold hue applied sparingly for luxurious touches and fine decorative details throughout the interface.
- Headlines: 'Playfair' (Serif). This elegant, high-contrast serif font captures the required high-end, romantic, and fashionable feel for all page titles and major headings.
- Body Text: 'PT Sans' (Sans-serif). A clean, modern humanist sans-serif, chosen for its excellent readability and contemporary feel, used for all body content and descriptive text.
- Logo: The brand name 'InFaith Journey' will use the custom, elegant cursive script and small chapel/arch icon graphic exactly as specified, integrated as a visual asset rather than a dynamic font.
- Imagery Style: All photographic content will strictly adhere to the high-end romantic wedding aesthetic, featuring couples under floral arches, cherry-blossom overlays, soft golden-hour lighting, warm sepia filters, and bokeh effects. Custom brand icons will reflect the specified chapel/arch motif.
- Spacious & Responsive Layout: The multi-page website will feature a fully responsive design emphasizing generous white space, elegant rounded corners, and soft shadows for a luxurious, feminine presentation. Page layouts will meticulously replicate the 6 provided screenshot references for exact visual fidelity, including structured navigation, sidebars, and grid views.
- Smooth Interaction Animations: Subtle hover effects and fluid transitions will be applied to all interactive elements such as cards, buttons, and navigation links, enhancing the premium user experience and visual flow.
